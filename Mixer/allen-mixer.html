dLive S7000

Surface for dLive Digital Mixing System

The dLive S7000 is the flagship control surface of the dLive System, purpose-built for the most demanding touring and broadcast productions. It pairs a fully assignable, high-resolution control layout with dLive's ultra-low-latency Class-Compliant Gigabit stagerack architecture, supporting up to 128 processing channels and 64 mix buses.

Digital

DM48

Digital Mix Rack for dLive Digital Mixing System

The DM48 is a compact digital mix rack for the dLive System, delivering 48 processing channels and 24 mix buses with local mic preamps and I/O, pairing with any dLive surface over a Gigabit connection for a scalable, road-ready system footprint. It includes RackUltra FX: cutting edge technology, and next-generation effects, adding dedicated extra effects processing power on top of the console's standard engine so more reverbs, delays, and other effects can run simultaneously without taking processing power away from the main mix.

SQ-6

48 channel / 36 bus digital mixer

SQ-6 is a next generation digital mixer, powered by Allen & Heath's revolutionary XCVI 96kHz FPGA engine. Created to excel in demanding scenarios such as AV, corporate events, live productions and houses of worship, SQ-6 offers class-leading high-resolution audio fidelity and an ultra-low latency of 0.7ms. The console features 24 exceptional onboard mic preamps, plus 8 stereo FX engines with dedicated return channels.
