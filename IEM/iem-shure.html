Transmitter

P10T

Wireless Transmitter

Shure's P10T is a networkable, full-rack, dual-channel wireless transmitter used in conjunction with the P10R Wireless Bodypack Receiver as part of the PSM 1000 Personal Monitor System. It combines state-of-the-art technology with touring-grade features for professional monitoring applications of any size, offering a 72 MHz to 80 MHz tuning bandwidth and networked remote control via Wireless Workbench software.

Transmitter

ADTQ

Axient® Digital PSM Quad Transmitter

The ADTQ is the quad-channel base station transmitter for the Axient Digital PSM wireless personal monitor system, delivering up to four independent digital IEM mixes from a single rack space with best-in-class RF performance for the most demanding touring and broadcast environments.

Receiver

ADXR

Axient® Digital PSM Beltpack Receiver

The ADXR is the beltpack receiver for the Axient Digital PSM wireless personal monitor system, pairing with the ADTQ transmitter to deliver a pristine digital IEM mix with extended battery life and the rugged, touring-grade build performers depend on.

Receiver

P10R

Diversity Wireless Bodypack Receiver

Diversity wireless bodypack receiver offers premium features for use with the PSM 1000 In-Ear Personal Monitoring System. Includes RF Scan, twin-antenna diversity stereo encoding, CueMode for monitoring different mixes, and MixMode Technology.

Receiver
