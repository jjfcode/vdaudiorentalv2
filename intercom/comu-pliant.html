CCU-44

CrewCom 4+4 Control Unit 900MHz

CrewNet is a robust, proprietary network designed specifically for the demands of critical communication environments. It is the backbone for interconnection between CrewCom devices, capable of deploying a system over a very large coverage area with Cat 5e (up to 100 m) and/or single mode fiber (up to 10 km).

Wireless Intercom

CRP-44-900

CrewCom 900MHz 4-Vol/4-Conf

The CrewCom Radio Pack is a highly designed and innovative digital wireless beltpack used for connecting mobile users to the CrewCom system. Along with standard full duplex communications, it combines flexible conference access and customizable controls for a highly functional yet familiar user experience.

Wireless Intercom

CRT-900

CrewCom 900MHz Radio Transceiver

The CrewCom Radio Transceiver houses a radio with corresponding antennas and serves as an access point enabling RF communications with CrewCom Radio Packs. Using CrewNet as a backbone, up to 46 RTs can be positioned on the network throughout a wide coverage area, connected directly, through hubs, or in a daisy-chain configuration.

CrewCom 8-Port Copper Hub

The CrewCom Hub supplies a total of eight CrewNet ports to allow extended interconnection for a variety of CrewCom devices.
