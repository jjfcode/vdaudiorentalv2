RNDI

Active Transformer Direct Interface

The RNDI is a single channel active direct box built around a custom Rupert Neve-designed transformer, delivering the same silky highs and rich low end found in Neve's classic console mic preamps. Rugged road-ready construction and phantom or battery power make it a touring and studio favorite.

Active DI

RNDI-M

Active Transformer Direct Interface

The RNDI-M brings the same custom Neve transformer-driven tone as the RNDI to a compact single-channel format, offering pristine active DI performance for stage and studio with switchable ground lift and ultra-low noise circuitry.

Rackmount DI

RNDI-8

8-Channel Rackmount Direct Interface

The RNDI-8 packs eight channels of Rupert Neve Designs' signature transformer-based DI circuitry into a single rack space, delivering consistent, musical direct injection across every channel for touring and installed systems.
