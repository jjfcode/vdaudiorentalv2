Our Story

A trusted resource for audio rental solutions

VD Audio Rental was founded in 2009 with the goal of becoming a trusted resource for live production and concert touring companies. Since then, we have supported a wide range of productions locally and abroad.

Along with maintaining a large inventory of professional audio gear, we focus on building strong client relationships and providing practical support for demanding production needs.

Our team brings real-world live production knowledge to every project, helping clients find dependable gear, responsive support, and quick solutions when they matter most.
