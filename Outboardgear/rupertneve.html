Dynamics

5045

Primary Source Enhancer

The 5045 Primary Source Enhancer is exceptionally useful at reducing feedback without negatively impacting the sonic integrity of the source signal, effectively increasing the level a microphone can be raised before feedback occurs in a live sound environment by up to 20dB. With controls that are very simply laid out and generally require minimal adjustment, the 5045 is an invaluable tool for churches, stadiums, performance halls, or any venue where feedback is problematic.

Dynamics

Portico II

Master Buss Processor

The Portico II Master Buss Processor is a creative tool that redefines the boundaries and limitations of traditional 2-channel compression and limiting. Though originally developed for mastering applications, the MBP quickly became a favorite of mix engineers, tracking engineers, and even FOH engineers. From mixing bass-heavy EDM to a delicate master of a chamber ensemble, it has something to offer everyone – no matter the use or genre.

Shelford Channel

Mic Pre + Inductor EQ + Compressor

The Shelford Channel is built around Rupert Neve's first new transformer-gain, class-A microphone preamplifier, a "best-of-the-classics" inductor EQ section, a tone-packed diode bridge compressor, the power of variable Silk saturation, a new dual-tap transformer output stage, and twice the operating voltage of vintage designs.

Shelford Dual Mic Pre 5025

Two-Channel Microphone Preamplifier

The 5025 Shelford Dual Mic Pre pairs two of Rupert Neve's transformer-coupled, class-A microphone preamplifiers in a single rack space, with variable Silk saturation on each channel for warm, musical color on the way in, alongside switchable phantom power, polarity, and high-pass filtering.

Newton Channel

Mic Pre + Parametric EQ + Compressor

The Newton Channel combines a transformer-coupled, class-A microphone preamplifier with a musical parametric EQ and a versatile compressor in a single rack space, bringing Rupert Neve's signature tone to a compact, affordable channel strip for tracking and mixing.

The 542 Tape Emulator recreates the harmonic saturation, compression, and warmth of analog tape without the noise, wow, flutter, or maintenance of a real tape machine, adding vintage character to vocals, drums, buses, and full mixes at the turn of a knob.
