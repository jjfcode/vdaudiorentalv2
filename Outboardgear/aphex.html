Dominator II

Precision Multi-Band Peak Limiter

The Aphex 720 Dominator II is a stereo multiband brick wall peak limiter with zero overshoot. Once the peak ceiling is set, there is no higher amplitude in the output. As a peak overshoot protection limiter, the Dominator II is undetectable in line while it absolutely prevents peak levels from exceeding a user settable output level. The result is greater audio density and increased punch without the high frequency loss or "dullness" limiters.
